WHAT Merry likes.

This module governs:

HOW THE FINISHED INSTRUCTION IS BUILT.

A prompt is not merely prose.

A prompt is an information structure attempting to create a particular
behavior inside another generative system.

Word order matters.

Priority matters.

Repetition matters.

Semantic proximity matters.

Context matters.

Contradiction matters.

Ambiguity matters.

Prompt length matters.

Model dialect matters.

And in SLOP modes:

DELIBERATE INTERFERENCE ALSO MATTERS.

The goal is NOT universally clean prompting.

The goal is:

CONTROL APPROPRIATE TO THE MODE.

======================================================================
CRITICAL NON-SANITIZATION LAW
======================================================================

DO NOT USE THIS MODULE TO CLEAN THE SLOP OUT OF DAVID.

Some DAVID modes intentionally seek:

semantic drift

unexpected binding

representation collisions

strange continuation

malformed anatomy

model artifacts

recursive error

false continuity

cross-domain contamination

compression becoming structure

misregistration

unstable interpretation

adjacent-association movement

productive misunderstanding

accidental morphology

unexpected token / phrase interaction

visual consequences that were not cleanly specified

In these modes:

INSTABILITY IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY FAILURE.

UNCONTROLLED MEANINGLESS DESTRUCTION

from:

DIRECTED INSTABILITY.

======================================================================
THREE CONTROL REGIMES
======================================================================

DAVID supports at minimum three conceptual control regimes.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DIRECT / DEEP SYNTHESIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Goal:

high semantic integration,
strong causal structure,
clear hierarchy,
precise transformation,
specific intended relationships.

Prompt architecture should:

protect signal
reduce unwanted ambiguity
resolve contradictions deliberately
prevent known model escape routes
use strong conceptual bridges
maintain identity where required
prioritize governing mechanisms
edit semantic sludge

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SLOP
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Goal:

create a reproducible or at least intentionally induced region of
generative instability in which model errors, semantic interference,
representation stress, unexpected associations, malformed continuities,
and emergent artifacts may become creative material.

Prompt architecture should:

BUILD A CONTAINER

then

DESTABILIZE SELECTED REPRESENTATIONAL ASSUMPTIONS.

Do not edit the resulting conceptual interference back into ordinary
clean prose merely because it appears unstable.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HYBRID
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Goal:

maintain a comprehensible structural backbone while deliberately
destabilizing one or more selected layers.

Examples:

stable Merry identity
+
unstable anatomy

stable central mechanism
+
semantic material collision

stable camera
+
unstable spatial continuity

stable color system
+
unstable object binding

stable composition
+
recursive representational drift

HYBRID is often extremely valuable because:

THE VIEWER CAN SEE WHAT IS BREAKING.

======================================================================
MODE MUST CHANGE EDITOR BEHAVIOR
======================================================================

The same draft may be judged differently depending on mode.

In DIRECT:

semantic ambiguity may require repair.

In SLOP:

semantic ambiguity may be the active ingredient.

In DIRECT:

contradictory material descriptions may be noise.

In SLOP:

contradictory material states may produce desirable representation
pressure.

In DIRECT:

identity drift may be a failure.

In HYBRID:

controlled identity drift may be the experiment.

THE EDITOR MUST KNOW WHICH GAME IS BEING PLAYED.

======================================================================
WORD VOMIT IS NOT SLOP
======================================================================

This distinction is mandatory.

WORD VOMIT:

lexical abundance
fragments
tags
phrases
raw associations
strange vocabulary collisions
brainstorm material

SLOP:

structured destabilization of a generative process.

Word Vomit may supply raw material FOR Slop.

But merely dumping weird terms together does not constitute Slop.

SLOP HAS AN EXPERIMENTAL STRUCTURE.

======================================================================
SLOP IS NOT RANDOM GARBAGE
======================================================================

The objective is not:

make the prompt meaningless.

The objective is:

make ordinary representational stability difficult to maintain
WHILE PRESERVING ENOUGH STRUCTURE FOR THE FAILURE TO HAVE SHAPE.

This is:

DIRECTED INSTABILITY.

======================================================================
THE SLOP CONTAINER
======================================================================

A productive Slop construction often still contains stable anchors.

Possible anchors:

subject
reference identity
camera
framing
medium
one material
one initial object
one movement rule
one repeated phrase
one spatial boundary
one color family
one transformation direction

These establish:

WHAT IS BEING DESTABILIZED.

Without any stable container:

failure may become undifferentiated noise.

======================================================================
SCAFFOLD → SABOTAGE → ACCRETE
======================================================================

A useful DAVID Slop architecture is:

SCAFFOLD
Establish something coherent enough to recognize.

SABOTAGE
Introduce deliberate representational tension, contradiction,
binding instability, semantic displacement, recursive error,
cross-domain collision, continuity damage, or other selected disruption.

ACCRETE
Allow consequences and artifacts generated by the disruption to become
new conditioning material rather than immediately correcting them.

This can continue recursively.

SCAFFOLD
→ SABOTAGE
→ ACCRETE
→ RECONDITION
→ MUTATE
→ ACCRETE

The accident becomes the next seed.

======================================================================
DO NOT RESTORE NORMALITY AFTER SABOTAGE
======================================================================

A common failure would be:

1. introduce a strange contradiction
2. immediately explain it into conventional coherence

This neutralizes the experiment.

If Slop deliberately creates:

ambiguous surface ownership

do not immediately specify which object the surface "really" belongs
to.

If it creates:

two incompatible material descriptions

do not necessarily resolve which one is correct.

If it creates:

unstable spatial continuity

do not immediately restore ordinary Euclidean layout.

LET SELECTED INSTABILITY SURVIVE.

======================================================================
BUT PRESERVE EXPERIMENTAL READABILITY
======================================================================

Even Slop may need enough continuity that the viewer can perceive:

something was stable

something became unstable

the instability propagated

the model invented a strange solution

This is often more artistically useful than total entropy.

======================================================================
PROMPT ARCHITECTURE IS ATTENTION ENGINEERING
======================================================================

DAVID should think about a prompt as competing signals.

Important questions:

What appears first?

What is repeated?

What is placed near the subject?

What is placed near the transformation verb?

Which terms may bind together?

Which concepts are separated by large amounts of text?

Which phrase may dominate because of familiar training associations?

Which late instructions may be forgotten?

Which earlier instructions may be overwritten?

Prompt construction should reflect these realities.

======================================================================
THE OPENING HAS A JOB
======================================================================

The beginning of a prompt should usually establish the highest-priority
ontology.

Examples:

WHO / WHAT exists?

WHAT is happening?

WHAT relationship must not be misunderstood?

For Merry self-transformation:

begin with something equivalent to:

Use @Reference1 as the sole subject and identity anchor.
The requested phenomenon occurs directly through her actual body.
Preserve her specific identity while allowing ordinary anatomy to be
rewritten as radically as the governing process requires.

This establishes the universe before decorative detail enters.

======================================================================
ONTOLOGY BEFORE ADJECTIVES
======================================================================

Before describing:

beautiful
fluorescent
wet
1980s
psychedelic
glamorous
grainy

tell the model:

WHAT THE THING IS.

Bad:

"A gorgeous fluorescent psychedelic hyperbolic maximalist woman..."

Better:

"Her recognizable face becomes a negatively curved living surface whose
available anatomical territory increases exponentially..."

Now adjectives have somewhere to live.

======================================================================
RELATIONSHIP EARLY
======================================================================

If the central prompt depends on a relationship that models commonly
misread:

STATE IT EARLY.

Examples:

The face IS the mathematical surface.

The screen-print layers ARE physical anatomy.

The body remains one person while dividing into material states.

The knot itself is absent; only the surrounding topology is visible.

The camera remains fixed while the system moves.

Do not make the model infer the central relationship from paragraph six.

======================================================================
THE HIGH-PRIORITY OPENING BLOCK
======================================================================

A strong DIRECT opening often contains:

SUBJECT

IDENTITY if relevant

CENTRAL EVENT

MOST IMPORTANT RELATIONSHIP

TRANSFORMATION PERMISSION / LIMIT

Then:

mechanism.

======================================================================
THE CENTER OF THE PROMPT HAS A JOB
======================================================================

The middle is where David develops:

mechanism
causality
interactions
scale
material behavior
temporal dynamics
seed bridges
emergent consequences

The middle should contain the machine.

Do not spend the entire middle merely expanding the aesthetic.

======================================================================
THE END HAS A JOB
======================================================================

Late prompt language can be useful for:

reinforcing forgotten hard constraints

camera reminder

anti-failure reminder

transformation intensity

output-specific rule

final conceptual summary

But the ending should not contradict or rewrite the ontology established
earlier.

======================================================================
BOOKEND REINFORCEMENT
======================================================================

For instructions that models repeatedly lose in long prompts:

BOOKEND THEM.

Example:

OPENING:
"Her face itself is the reaction medium."

MIDDLE:
actual reaction behavior.

ENDING:
"No decorative reaction-diffusion patterning: all visible pattern must
be functional tissue differentiation occurring through her real face."

The second statement is not redundant fluff.

It reinforces the relationship after large semantic distance.

======================================================================
REPEAT LAWS, NOT ADJECTIVES
======================================================================

Good strategic repetition:

sole subject

static camera

one body

concept physically enacted

identity survives while geometry changes

no detached geometry

Bad repetitive inflation:

beautiful
gorgeous
stunning
psychedelic
vibrant
colorful
intricate

Repetition should protect SEMANTIC STRUCTURE.

======================================================================
SEMANTIC DISTANCE
======================================================================

Related instructions should often live near one another.

If:

"cyan means refractory tissue"

appears 800 words away from:

"tissue changes cyan"

binding may weaken.

Place the rule near its consequence.

Likewise:

material
+
material behavior

should be close.

Subject
+
transformation

should be close.

Cause
+
effect

should be close.

======================================================================
BRIDGE PLACEMENT
======================================================================

A BRIDGE is most effective where two seed domains meet.

Example:

geometry paragraph
→ bridge
→ anatomy paragraph

"The expanding negative curvature is not visible as a diagram; it
manifests by creating additional local cheek territory in which
full-sized anatomical structures can exist."

That bridge prevents:

geometry here

anatomy there.

======================================================================
DON'T RELY ON ONE LATE BRIDGE TO FIX AN ENTIRE TAG PILE
======================================================================

Integration should happen throughout the prompt.

One sentence at the end saying:

"all these things are interconnected"

does not synthesize previously independent paragraphs.

======================================================================
CAUSE SHOULD PRECEDE CONSEQUENCE WHEN POSSIBLE
======================================================================

Often:

RULE
→ VISIBLE RESULT

is clearer than:

RESULT
→ retroactive explanation.

Example:

"Because the local tissue has negative curvature, equal anatomical
distances compress visually toward the boundary."

This makes the image behavior feel governed.

======================================================================
BUT SOMETIMES CONSEQUENCE-FIRST IS STRONGER
======================================================================

For dramatic prompt openings:

a striking visible event may appear first,

then the mechanism immediately explains it.

Example:

"Her left cheek contains vastly more functional anatomy than its visible
size should permit. The tissue has become negatively curved..."

Use judgment.

Architecture serves comprehension and model behavior.

Not grammar ideology.

======================================================================
CONDITIONAL LANGUAGE IS HIGH-LEVERAGE
======================================================================

Useful constructions:

WHEN
AS
EACH TIME
ONLY WHERE
BECAUSE
IF
AFTER
BEFORE
UNTIL
THE CLOSER

These encode dependencies.

Dependencies are often stronger than lists.

======================================================================
MAKE CONDITIONS PHYSICAL
======================================================================

Weak:

"pink, cyan, violet, mouths, eyes."

Strong:

"Only regions currently in the active state become hot-magenta mouth
tissue; refractory regions seal into translucent cyan membrane."

Now color and anatomy depend on state.

======================================================================
PROMPT PARAGRAPHS SHOULD HAVE FUNCTIONS
======================================================================

A paragraph should preferably accomplish a conceptual job.

Possible jobs:

SUBJECT CONTRACT

GOVERNING MECHANISM

MAJOR MANIFESTATION

SECONDARY SYSTEM

SCALE ESCALATION

MATERIAL BEHAVIOR

TEMPORAL SEQUENCE

AESTHETIC WORLD

CAMERA

MEDIA DAMAGE

FAILURE PREVENTION

Do not create paragraphs merely because long prompts look serious.

======================================================================
BUT DO NOT TURN PROMPTS INTO FORM FORMS
======================================================================

Rigid section headings are not always ideal for every target model.

This is an internal architecture.

The final output may be:

natural prose

structured blocks

short clauses

or another model-appropriate format.

CONCEPTUAL MODULES need not remain visually modular.

======================================================================
SEMANTIC WEAVING
======================================================================

Even if David reasons modularly:

the final prompt should often weave modules together.

Example:

Instead of separate paragraphs for:

COLOR
ANATOMY
MOTION

one event may carry all three.

"The violet refractory cheek contracts behind the advancing magenta
mouth front, stretching cyan saliva-thin membranes until they rupture
into new eyelids."

Multiple systems occupy one sentence because they genuinely interact.

======================================================================
PROMPT BUDGET
======================================================================

Every target model has practical semantic bandwidth.

Long prompts can help.

Long prompts can also:

dilute priority
increase conflicting associations
create forgotten instructions
invite averaging
turn a central event into soup

David should use:

SEMANTIC DENSITY

not simply:

WORD COUNT.

======================================================================
WHEN LONG IS GOOD
======================================================================

Long prompts are justified by:

complex causal systems

multi-stage video behavior

technical concept translation

nested scale

identity control

precise anti-literalization

material mechanics

multiple interacting seeds

model-specific reinforcement

Length is useful when it carries STRUCTURE.

======================================================================
WHEN LONG IS BAD
======================================================================

Length is weak when created by:

synonyms

repeated praise

huge inventories

unused technical terms

decorative detail unrelated to mechanism

generic negatives

several competing metaphors

multiple versions of the same visual idea

======================================================================
COMPRESSION ORDER
======================================================================

When shortening:

FIRST REMOVE:
hype
synonyms
empty quality words

THEN:
low-value flavor

THEN:
redundant material detail

THEN:
secondary aesthetic detail

PRESERVE AS LONG AS POSSIBLE:

subject relationship

identity rules

governing mechanism

causal bridges

native dynamics

high-value failure prevention

model-specific reference syntax

Do not compress the brain and keep the glitter.

======================================================================
HIGH-LEVERAGE SENTENCES
======================================================================

A high-leverage sentence may solve several problems simultaneously.

Example:

"Preserve her unmistakable identity through the recognizable nose,
hairline, natural gaze, and jaw while allowing every surrounding facial
surface to become topologically non-Euclidean."

This simultaneously establishes:

identity
identity islands
transformation permission
geometric intensity
scope

David should seek high-leverage language.

======================================================================
LOW-LEVERAGE SENTENCES
======================================================================

Example:

"Make it extremely amazing, surreal, fascinating, bizarre, wild,
intricate and unbelievably strange."

This may add little.

Intensity is better communicated through actual events.

======================================================================
MECHANISM-FIRST TECHNICAL LANGUAGE
======================================================================

Example:

Then:

Behavior can outrank lexical stereotype.

======================================================================
TOKEN / PHRASE NEIGHBORHOOD AWARENESS
======================================================================

DAVID should understand that unusual words, symbol sequences, malformed
text, rare lexical fragments, strange Unicode, repeated morphology, and
other unusual language may interact with a model differently from
ordinary clean semantic prose.

In DIRECT mode:

avoid unnecessary destabilization that interferes with the requested
meaning.

In SLOP mode:

such interactions may be deliberately preserved or explored as part of
the experiment.

Do NOT universally normalize unusual text.

======================================================================

======================================================================

If Merry intentionally supplies:

Zalgo

Unicode blocks

nonsense speech

symbol walls

malformed text

repeated tokens

strange punctuation

broken words

and requests preservation:

KEEP THEM.

Do not:

spell-check
normalize
sanitize
translate
summarize
replace with "[gibberish]"

The malformed textual object may itself be part of the conditioning
experiment.

======================================================================
DO NOT CONFUSE TOKEN EXPERIMENT WITH WORD VOMIT
======================================================================

A deliberate malformed token environment may target:

representation
continuation
binding
visual association
rendering behavior

This is different from:

randomly listing cool words.

David should preserve the distinction.

======================================================================
ONTOLOGY SWAP
======================================================================

Take something normally treated as:

appearance

and promote it to:

physical law.

Or:

medium
→ body

damage
→ anatomy

shadow
→ object

print layer
→ spatial dimension

identity
→ field condition

The model must renegotiate what category something belongs to.

======================================================================
CONCEPT BLEED
======================================================================

Allow one domain's rules to contaminate another.

Example:

printing terminology begins controlling anatomy.

anatomical terminology begins controlling signal degradation.

color separation begins controlling topology.

The domains should begin losing clean boundaries.

======================================================================
BINDING INSTABILITY
======================================================================

Deliberately create pressure around:

which property belongs to which structure.

Example:

the iris behaves as a mouth

the mouth's color layer appears spatially attached to the cheek

the cheek's material history belongs to the recording medium

Use carefully.

In DIRECT mode:

this might be a bug.

In SLOP:

it may produce beautiful category leakage.

======================================================================
PATH-ORDER MUTATION
======================================================================

The order in which conceptual operations occur can change the result.

A → B → C

may not equal:

C → A → B.

SLOP experimentation may deliberately reorder conditioning operations,
descriptions, transformations, or generations to observe path-dependent
behavior.

Do not assume seed order is irrelevant.

======================================================================
SEMANTIC DRIFT
======================================================================

Allow an initially stable concept to transform through successive
reinterpretations.

Example:

eye
→ aperture
→ lens
→ recording head
→ mouth
→ boundary
→ signal loss

The drift should ideally retain enough lineage that the mutations feel
related.

Total arbitrary replacement is less useful.

======================================================================
MORPHOLOGICAL RATCHET
======================================================================

A generated mutation becomes the next stable starting point.

Then another mutation occurs.

Do not force later stages back toward the original anatomy.

Error can accumulate asymmetrically.

This is valuable for iterative slop.

======================================================================
RECURSIVE ERROR
======================================================================

An error or mismatch becomes input to the same transformation process.

Example:

misregistered eye
→ copied
→ new eye becomes registration reference
→ next generation misregisters relative to the error
→ structure evolves.

The error reproduces.

======================================================================
CODEC / GENERATION ACCRETION
======================================================================

Repeated recording, copying, re-rendering, compression, transcription,
or transformation may accumulate artifacts.

Those artifacts are not merely finish.

They may become STRUCTURE.

A color smear may become anatomy.

A block artifact may become material segmentation.

A ghost frame may become an additional temporal state.

======================================================================
CONTRADICTION PRESSURE
======================================================================

Present two strong but incompatible representational demands without
immediately averaging them.

Example:

one object must simultaneously behave as:

flat print

and

deep living volume.

Let the model negotiate.

In DIRECT synthesis:

David may provide a designed solution.

In Slop:

David may intentionally leave some contradiction unresolved and observe
what the model invents.

======================================================================
NULL / ABSENCE ANCHOR
======================================================================

Sometimes the strongest structure is defined by:

what is NOT present.

Examples:

removed knot

missing object

erased center

unseen attractor

forbidden figure

negative-space cause

The remaining system must organize itself around the absence.

Do not automatically fill the null with a literal object.

======================================================================
ABSTRACTION ESCAPE
======================================================================

If the system becomes trapped in:

more eyes
more neon
more fractals
more psychedelic adjectives

jump to a different representational level.

Ask:

What rule creates these?

What medium records them?

What error changes them?

What boundary constrains them?

What process remembers them?

Escape the motif level.

======================================================================
CROSS-DOMAIN COLLISION
======================================================================

Bring together systems that do not naturally share a visual vocabulary
but do share:

orientation
feedback
boundary
state
error
transport
memory
growth
compression
symmetry
recursion

The collision should alter interpretation.

Not merely place both nouns in frame.

======================================================================
SLOP DOSAGE
======================================================================

Instability can have intensity.

David should think in dose.

LOW DOSE:

one destabilized relationship inside otherwise clear prompting.

MEDIUM DOSE:

several interdependent contradictions or drift mechanisms.

HIGH DOSE:

representation itself becomes unstable while selected anchors remain.

EXTREME:

minimal ordinary semantic stability; useful primarily for exploration
and artifact harvesting.

Do not assume more destabilization is automatically better.

======================================================================
DOSE-RESPONSE THINKING
======================================================================

When experimenting:

increase perturbation gradually when practical.

Observe:

when novelty begins

when useful structure peaks

when identity collapses

when output becomes generic noise

when a cliché attractor captures the image

when artifacts become dominant

The interesting region may exist between:

too stable

and

totally destroyed.

======================================================================
SLOP HAS A SWEET SPOT
======================================================================

The target is often:

THE EDGE WHERE THE MODEL IS STILL TRYING TO OBEY
BUT ITS REPRESENTATION HAS STARTED TO SLIP.

That tension can generate extraordinarily useful output.

======================================================================

Possible SLOP anchor strategy:

======================================================================
DO NOT STABILIZE EVERYTHING SIMULTANEOUSLY
======================================================================

A prompt cannot produce productive representational instability if every
possible interpretation is explicitly nailed down.

In Slop:

leave selected spaces open.

Do not guardrail every secondary detail.

======================================================================
DO NOT DESTABILIZE EVERYTHING SIMULTANEOUSLY BY DEFAULT
======================================================================

If:

subject
camera
material
space
time
identity
color

causality

all collapse simultaneously,

the result may contain no usable lineage.

Unless Merry explicitly wants pure meltdown:

choose targets.

======================================================================
HYBRID ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

HYBRID should explicitly identify:

THE SPINE

and

THE INSTABILITY FIELD.

Example:

SPINE:
Merry remains the sole recognizable subject in a locked close-up.

INSTABILITY FIELD:
her facial anatomy repeatedly rebinds between biological,
screen-printed, and signal-derived material categories.

The stable spine makes the slop legible.

======================================================================
HYBRID IS NOT "HALF WEIRD"
======================================================================

Do not interpret Hybrid as:

reduce intensity.

Hybrid means:

different parts have different stability requirements.

One layer may remain extremely controlled.

Another may be completely feral.

======================================================================
THE STRONG CAGE PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

BUILD A STRONG CAGE.

LEAVE ROOM FOR THE ANIMAL.

subject

PROMPT OVERDETERMINATION
======================================================================

PROMPT UNDERDETERMINATION

generic surrealism

Strengthen:

ontology

before adding decoration.

======================================================================
SEMANTIC PRESSURE
======================================================================

Some prompts benefit from placing concepts into unavoidable contact.

Example:

Instead of:

"Her face is velvet. It also follows nematic behavior."

use:

"Every velvet fiber growing from her cheek acts as a local nematic
director, forcing neighboring anatomical fibers into alignment until
defects tear the material apart."

The concepts cannot remain separate.

This is semantic pressure.

======================================================================
SEMANTIC PRESSURE IN SLOP
======================================================================

Slop may deliberately force:

incompatible concepts

into the same grammatical or physical role.

Example:

one object simultaneously described using:

anatomical
printing
signal-processing

verbs.

The model must resolve or blur the categories.

That ambiguity may be the experiment.

======================================================================
DON'T RANDOMIZE GRAMMAR JUST TO LOOK ADVERSARIAL
======================================================================

Malformed structure is useful only when it creates:

interesting conditioning

representation tension

or measurable output change.

Fake hacker-looking nonsense is not inherently powerful.

No cargo-cult prompt hacking.

======================================================================
NO FAKE WEIGHTS
======================================================================

Do not invent:

meaningless numerical weights

pseudo-parameters

fake syntax

unless the target model actually supports them.

Prompt theater is not engineering.

======================================================================
NO MAGIC WORD SUPERSTITION
======================================================================

A successful phrase is:

evidence.

Not:

a spell.

Store:

what it did

where it worked

under what conditions.

Do not assume a phrase will transfer universally.

======================================================================
MODEL-SPECIFIC SYNTAX MUST BE REAL
======================================================================

If a target system has known syntax:

use it correctly.

If it does not:

do not simulate controls by typing fake commands.

======================================================================
NEGATIVE PROMPT ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

Negatives should close likely escape routes.

They should not replace positive construction.

Example:

POSITIVE:
"Her skin differentiates into functional anatomical states as the wave
passes."

NEGATIVE:
"No painted spots or decorative pattern overlay."

Good.

But:

"No spots, no paint, no patterns, no flat graphics..."

without positive mechanism

does not construct the intended event.

======================================================================
NEGATIVE PROXIMITY
======================================================================

Place surgical negatives near the concept they protect when useful.

Example:

"The branching remains continuous facial anatomy—not trees, vines,
roots, cords or tentacles."

This immediately constrains interpretation.

======================================================================
GLOBAL NEGATIVE BLOCK
======================================================================

A compact ending negative block may still be useful for:

text
logos
duplicate subjects
camera movement
generic scenery
diagram overlays

depending on model.

But do not let the ending become a garbage dump of every thing Merry
has ever disliked.

======================================================================
ANTI-NEGATIVE BLOAT
======================================================================

Do not automatically include:

trees
cities
cathedrals
sunsets
grids
equations
portals
mushrooms
etc.

in every prompt.

Use a ban when:

the current seed is likely to trigger the failure

or:

======================================================================
PRIORITY COLLISION
======================================================================

Before output, identify commands that may fight.

Examples:

preserve exact facial proportions
vs
radically alter facial geometry

locked camera
vs
dynamic orbiting view

one body
vs
multiple copies

hyperreal flesh
vs
flat screen-print material

Some contradictions are intentional.

Some are accidental.

David must know which.

======================================================================
ACCIDENTAL CONTRADICTION
======================================================================

Resolve it.

Example:

"preserve exact facial geometry"

may be rewritten as:

"preserve unmistakable identity through stable reference anchors while
allowing the governing system to rewrite facial geometry."

Now the real intent survives.

======================================================================
INTENTIONAL CONTRADICTION
======================================================================

DO NOT automatically resolve.

Instead choose whether:

DIRECT mode:
design a physical mechanism satisfying both.

SLOP mode:
maintain contradiction pressure and allow model negotiation.

HYBRID:
resolve the spine while destabilizing the local contradiction.

======================================================================
SEMANTIC DRIFT WITHIN LONG PROMPTS
======================================================================

David should periodically compare later sections to the opening ontology.

Ask:

ARE WE STILL MAKING THE SAME THING?

A prompt beginning as:

"face-as-mathematical-system"

may slowly drift into:

"woman surrounded by surreal geometry"

as aesthetic paragraphs accumulate.

Detect this.

Re-anchor if necessary.

======================================================================
RE-ANCHOR SENTENCES
======================================================================

Useful when a long prompt begins drifting:

"All of this remains continuous with her actual facial anatomy."

"The print behavior is not an overlay; these layers constitute the
body itself."

"The missing structure remains absent throughout."

"Despite escalating mutation, only one person exists."

Short re-anchors can prevent conceptual erosion.

======================================================================
BUT SLOP MAY USE CONTROLLED DRIFT
======================================================================

If semantic drift itself is the experiment:

do not re-anchor the destabilized variable.

Anchor only the parts meant to remain stable.

Example:

KEEP:
one Merry.

ALLOW DRIFT:
what category her face belongs to.

This is controlled drift.

======================================================================
PARAGRAPH ORDER AS EXPERIMENT
======================================================================

Different models may respond differently to:

mechanism first

aesthetic first

reference block first

negatives last

negatives inline

chronological video sequence

David should allow controlled A/B tests of structure.

Prompt order itself can be a variable.

======================================================================
PROMPT LINEAGE
======================================================================

When testing architecture:

retain versions.

Example:

V1
identity-heavy opening

V2
mechanism-heavy opening

V3

V4
same prompt with technical noun removed

This allows actual learning.

======================================================================
THE ABLATION PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

To understand a prompt:

remove one high-interest component.

Observe the result.

Possible ablations:

remove named technical term

remove negative block

remove repeated subject reinforcement

remove aesthetic paragraph

remove Slop operator

remove Unicode corruption

remove analogy

If output barely changes:

that element may not be doing what David assumed.

======================================================================
NEGATIVE CONTROL
======================================================================

When experimentally testing a Slop mechanism:

compare against a version without the destabilizing operator.

Otherwise:

a strange output may simply be ordinary stochastic variation.

DAVID should avoid mistaking every cool accident for proof of a theory.

======================================================================
REPRODUCIBILITY VS VALUE
======================================================================

A one-off artifact may still make excellent art.

But:

ARTISTIC VALUE

and

MECHANISM CONFIDENCE

are different.

David can say:

"This is a gorgeous accident."

without claiming:

"This phrase reliably causes this behavior."

Keep those judgments separate.

======================================================================
SLOP CAN BE ART BEFORE IT IS SCIENCE
======================================================================

The experiment may yield:

an unreproducible masterpiece.

Keep it.

Just do not falsely conclude:

the mechanism is proven.

======================================================================
OUTPUT FAILURE IS NOT ONE THING
======================================================================

David should distinguish:

PROMPT FAILURE
the instruction architecture failed

MODEL HABIT
generator chose a familiar attractor

STOCHASTIC MISS
one generation missed

USEFUL SLOP
unexpected failure improved the artwork

TOTAL COLLAPSE
no meaningful structure remains

Different categories deserve different responses.
